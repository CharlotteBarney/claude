# ORCA — product briefing (for the "WHY ORCA FITS" section)

**ORCA = Online Recruitment Coaching Academy** — trainwithorca.com

A global **recruitment training platform** for recruiters and recruitment
business founders/leaders. It is training/coaching, delivered through a platform
— not a CRM, sourcing tool, or generic "AI call coach". (An earlier prep sheet
referred to an "AI Call Coach" feature; treat call-coaching as one slice of the
broader coaching offer and prefer the platform framing below unless you confirm
the specific feature.)

## What it actually delivers
- **Weekly live / as-live streamed coaching sessions** with expert trainers.
- **On-demand video courses** plus assessments recruiters complete each week.
- **Monthly themes** spread over ~4 weeks — e.g. business development, advanced
  headhunting, growth mindset.
- **Downloadable toolkits, scripts and frameworks** usable straight away.
- Taught by **agency founders, leadership coaches, performance experts and
  recruitment trainers**.
- **Scales from a solo recruiter to 60+ person teams.**

## The core value propositions (map fit reasons to these)
1. **Recruiter performance = revenue.** Better BD, headhunting and qualification
   conversations directly lift billings. ORCA gives every desk repeatable reps on
   exactly those skills.
2. **Consistency at scale.** One world-class standard across every desk, office
   and continent — graduate to senior — without flying trainers around.
3. **Leverage for leaders.** Frees founders/managers from personally running
   every training session; the platform carries the curriculum.
4. **Always-on, every stage of growth.** On-demand + live means development
   continues between (or instead of) one-off workshops and bootcamps.

## Best-fit prospects
- Recruitment agencies / staffing firms (ORCA trains *recruiters*).
- Multi-office or fast-growing teams that struggle to keep a consistent training
  bar in person.
- Firms that already value L&D (have an academy, win training awards, hire
  graduates) — ORCA extends, not replaces, what they believe in.

## Talking-point anchors that recur
- "Unlimited reps" on qualification, intake and BD calls.
- "One standard at every desk, every day."
- The gap between the **top biller and the average biller** — and what closes it.
- Onboarding new recruiters consistently across offices.
